Highlights

  • Most wins at Sutton Park: Belgrave Harriers with 7.
  • Fastest team: Aldershot Farnham & District AC 'A' ran 1:42:44 in 2021.
  • Fastest leg: C Davies (Telford AC) ran 16:25 on leg 2 in 2003. That was 35 seconds faster than the next runner that day (R Whalley, 17:00), so it may be a timing error, but it is the official result. Next fastest: C Stephenson 16:28 (1999) and Andrew Butchart 16:29 (2016).
  • 1999 was unusually fast: 13 legs under 17:00, against 0-3 in most years. Its top 20 legs averaged 16:50, against a typical 17:15. That points to conditions or a slight course variation, and 1999 teams take several places in the all-time top 50.
  • Faster in recent years: the average winning time for 2021-2024 was 1:43:38, against 1:45:44 for 2010-2019. The 2024 race had 9 sub-17 legs.
  • Biggest field: 82 complete teams in 2017.
  • New course records (2025 onwards): Cambridge & Coleridge AC 'A' ran 1:35:21 (2026). Fastest leg: Will Barnicoat (Aldershot Farnham & District AC) 15:10, leg 6, 2026.

Notes and caveats

  • Source: race-results.co.uk team results pages (1998-2024), the official 2025 results PDF, and RaceTec for 2026.
  • The source pages cut runner names to 16 characters and club names to about 30. Club names are standardised across years: 516 printed spellings map to about 230 clubs. Renamed clubs are combined, for example Blackheath Harriers into Blackheath & Bromley, Derby & County into Derby AC, Puma TVH into Thames Valley, and Brighton & Hove City into Brighton & Hove AC.
  • Individual rankings include legs run for incomplete teams and non-counting guest teams, since those are valid timed runs. Legs from disqualified teams are excluded, as is the unplaced 2026 Invicta East Kent team.
  • Runner-level statistics such as appearances and fastest-of-day counts match runners on first initial plus surname within a club. This is approximate: two runners with the same initial and surname at one club would be merged.
  • 2025 leg positions are calculated from cumulative times because the PDF doesn't print them. 2025 and 2026 leg times are rounded to the second, so they can differ from the official team total by a second or two.
